Walk through a room. Get a 3D model you can measure.

Film a slow walk around a room. video-to-3d rebuilds it as a 3D point cloud in real-world metres, with its size, a floor plan and its colours.

Ready to send pictures?

Your video stays on this device. These pictures were taken out of it here, in your browser.

    • What is sent: JPEG pictures, in total, each at most 1024 px wide.
    • What is not sent: the video, sound, and the video's location and camera details. The pictures were redrawn, which removes that information.
    • Where it goes: . That server works out where the camera was.
    • What the server keeps: nothing. It deletes the pictures when it finishes and does not log them.

    The pictures show the inside of your home. Only continue if you trust that server.
